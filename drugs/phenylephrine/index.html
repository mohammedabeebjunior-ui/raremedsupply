Phenylephrine: how to take it safely

Sudafed PE

Reviewed by Pharm. Abeeb Junior

What it is for

Relieves a blocked nose and sinuses from colds and flu.

How to take it

Swallow with water.

When to take it

Take it every 4 hours when needed, up to the label maximum.

Take it with

With or without food.

What to avoid

  • Using it for more than 5 to 7 days.
  • Tell your doctor if you have high blood pressure, heart disease or thyroid problems.
  • Taking it with other cold products with the same ingredient.

Possible side effects

Trouble sleeping, nervousness, headache, mild stomach upset.

Get help if

Chest pain, a very fast heartbeat or severe headache: get urgent help.
