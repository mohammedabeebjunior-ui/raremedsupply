Artemether-Lumefantrine: how to take it safely

Coartem, Lonart, Amatem

What it is for

Treats uncomplicated malaria.

How to take it

  • Take the full course exactly as shown on the pack. The usual course is 6 doses over 3 days.
  • Swallow with water.
  • If you vomit within 1 hour of a dose, tell your pharmacist, as the dose may need to be repeated.

When to take it

  • Take the first dose, then the second dose 8 hours later, then one dose in the morning and one in the evening for the next 2 days.
  • Do not skip doses.

Take it with

With food or a milky drink, as this helps your body absorb the medicine.

What to avoid

  • Do not stop early, even if the fever goes away.
  • Tell your pharmacist or doctor if you are pregnant, breastfeeding, or taking other medicines, especially for HIV, TB or heart rhythm.

Possible side effects

Headache, dizziness, weakness, nausea, loss of appetite.

Get help if

  • Get urgent help for fainting, a racing or irregular heartbeat, confusion, repeated vomiting, or if you cannot keep the tablets down.
  • See a doctor if you are not better after 2 to 3 days.
