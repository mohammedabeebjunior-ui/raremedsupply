Lidocaine-prilocaine cream: how to take it safely

EMLA

Reviewed by Pharm. Abeeb Junior

What it is for

Numbs the skin before injections, drips and small procedures.

How to take it

  • Apply a thick layer on clean, intact skin and cover with a dressing.
  • Do not rub it in.

When to take it

Apply 1 to 2 hours before the procedure.

Take it with

Not applicable.

What to avoid

  • Broken skin, or near the eyes or ears.
  • Using it on large areas or for longer than advised.
  • Tell your doctor if you or your baby has a blood condition called methaemoglobinaemia, or if you are pregnant.

Possible side effects

Skin paleness, redness, itching, mild burning.

Get help if

Blue or grey lips or skin, severe breathlessness or a rash: get urgent help.
